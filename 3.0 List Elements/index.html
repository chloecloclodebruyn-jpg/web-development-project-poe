Soft Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients

Dough

  • 240 ml warm milk
  • 7 g active dry yeast
  • 50 g granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg and 1 egg yolk
  • 60 g softened butter
  • 400 g bread flour

Cinnamon filling

  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • 45 g softened butter

Method

  1. Stir the warm milk, yeast, and sugar together. Let stand until foamy.
  2. Mix in the egg, egg yolk, butter, and flour to form a soft dough.
  3. Knead for about 8 minutes, then cover and let rise until doubled.
  4. Roll the dough into a rectangle and spread with butter, sugar, and cinnamon.
  5. Roll up, slice into even pieces, and arrange in a baking dish.
  6. Let rise again, then bake at 180°C until golden.
